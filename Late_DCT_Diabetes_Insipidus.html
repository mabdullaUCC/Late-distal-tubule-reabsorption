Liddle syndrome — consequences of constitutively active ENaC
↑ Na⁺ reabsorptionNa⁺ and water retention → extracellular volume expansion
↑ Blood pressureVolume-dependent hypertension
↑ K⁺ secretionHypokalaemia
↑ H⁺ secretionMetabolic alkalosis
↓ Renin · ↓ AldosteroneSuppressed by volume expansion
ENaC is overactive independently of aldosterone. Amiloride or triamterene inhibits ENaC; spironolactone is generally ineffective.

↓ Water reabsorptionWater remains in the tubular lumen

Central DI: insufficient ADH secretion; responds to desmopressin. Nephrogenic DI: impaired renal response to ADH; desmopressin has little or no effect. This diagram illustrates their shared final water-transport defect.
